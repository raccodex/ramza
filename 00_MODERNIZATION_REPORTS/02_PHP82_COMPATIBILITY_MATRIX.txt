1. COMPATIBILITY GATE
---------------------
The minimum-version declarations remain at PHP 7.1 during the audit. They will
not be raised to PHP 8.2 until executable first-party lint passes and available
runtime probes no longer produce PHP 8.2 fatal/deprecation failures.

Known minimum-version locations:
  assets/init.php: version_compare(PHP_VERSION, '7.1.0', '>=')
  install/index.php: requirements check and PHP 7.1+ requirement text
  admin-panel/pages/changelog/content.phtml already claims PHP 8.2 support; this
  claim is historical text and is not treated as test evidence.
